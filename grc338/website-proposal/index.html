Introduction

Rose City Roasters

Rose City Roasters is a small, independently owned café located in Portland, Oregon.Inspired by Portland’s nickname, “The City of Roses,” the café focuses on small-batch roasted coffee, seasonal brunch dishes, and house-made pastries. The café serves as both a neighborhood coffee shop and it's atmosphere blends modern industrial design with warm, natural materials inspired by the Pacific Northwest.

Target audience

The primary users of the Rose City Roasters website are Portland locals, including young professionals, college students, remote workers, and neighborhood residents who regularly visit coffee shops. The site will also be used by tourists exploring Portland who are looking for an authentic, locally owned café experience.

Their primary tasks when visiting the website are to view the menu, check prices, confirm hours of operation, and find the café’s location. Many users are deciding whether to visit in the moment, so they want quick access to essential information.

About

Rose City Roasters was founded in 2023 by two longtime Portland residents who wanted to create a space centered around thoughtful coffee and community connection. The café roasts its beans in small batches and partners with Oregon farms and local bakeries to source fresh ingredients. Sustainability and quality are at the heart of every decision, from compostable packaging to seasonal menu updates.

The goal of Rose City Roasters is simple: serve excellent coffee in a relaxed, neighborhood environment. Guests are encouraged to stay, connect, and feel at home while enjoying handcrafted drinks and comforting food.
